# v2026.11.24

Two new tabs have been added to the analysis window. Dummy Parse GCD analysis improvements. Certain views can now
be filtered for defensive ability usage.

An effort has been made to compile a comprehensive list of Defensive abilities to use as a reference, however it is likely some elements have been overlooked. Please report any missing defensive abilities, erroneous query results, or display or scaling issues on the discord.

## Overlays

- Overlays can now be resized by dragging from the right or bottom border of the window
- Mouse cursor now visually changes when hovering over a draggable location
- Overlays snap to each other's edges when moved or resized
- Style presets in overlay settings: Simple, Transparent, Compact, Rounded and Flat
- New corner radius setting for bars inside overlays (control corner rounding on timers, boss HP, + more). Boss HP and timer bars now share the 3px default, so they look slightly more (timers) or less (boss HP) rounded than before
- Leaving the Overlays page or switching profiles with unsaved changes prompts the user to save or discard them
- Metric bars default to 2px spacing

## Aggro (new Analysis tab)

Experimental for now - feedback appreciated

- Shows who every enemy was focused on over the course of the fight as well as taunts and threat drop events
- Always know who pulled first. Easily discover who is threat dropping during Brontes Burn.
- A table of each enemy's time on each player, and a timeline of every enemy's and player's target with taunts marked
- "Who held each enemy" lists every change of aggro: who took it, who lost it, how long they held it, and whether a taunt or a death caused it
- Taunts and threat drops are listed with what they did: pulled the enemy, was already on the taunter, or overlapped another taunt

## Raid Debuffs (new Analysis tab)

Experimental for now - feedback appreciated

- Uptime of each raid debuff on each enemy, with a timeline per debuff
- Lists who applied each debuff, who else in the group could have, and how much damage landed while it was missing

## Analysis

- Defensive Cooldowns can now be filtered for in the ability usage, effect uptime, charts table, and death recap with a Defensive filter
- Death recap lists the defensives the player pressed before dying
- Death recap summary table formatting improved
- Added Cleanses column to raid overview table, off by default
- Right clicking on entries in the Abilities, Effect Uptime, or Combat log pages will now allow you to copy the ability/effect id to clipboard
- Faction mirror names functionality extended to cover effects
- Disciplines with no unique skills are detected more reliably in PvP (Rage/Fury, Focus/Concentration)

## Training Dummy Parses

- GCD analysis now attempts to detect channeled abilities and lists potential clipped abilities
- GCD graphs are now interactive and highlight based on ability
- Show GCD to 3 decimal places

## Bugfix

- The interface now follows the desktop text size: tabs, tables and forms no longer cut off text when large text is on
- Overlays shown while editing keep the unsaved preview
- An effect with a target filter that only matches the caster no longer blocks other effects from the same ability
- The Personal overlay's default window fits its default stats
